# SafeFrame photo editor

SCOPE: The main desktop and mobile photo editing screen in `demo/app.py`.

MODE: Operate. The visitor selects or takes one photo, chooses one of three clear edits, creates the result, compares it with the original, and downloads it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat one photo edit as a short trip through a ticket wallet: choose a treatment, make the edit, collect the finished image. Refuse feed-like browsing and decorative upload-card stacks.

OWN-WORLD: Use carrier navy `#0D1B3D`, jet red `#D7262D`, coupon white `#FFFFFF`, stock white `#F2F2F0`, and carbon purple `#4B2E83`. Use crisp rules, tab-like mode labels, carbon-purple processing states, and a red ready/void mark. Keep photo controls standard and legible; the travel artifact contributes color, typography, density, and a single journey-status signature.

STORY: The visitor knows which photo is being edited, sees three distinct options, understands when generation is running, then sees and downloads the edited image beside the source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy top bar names SafeFrame. At desktop, the source photo and compact mode-and-action controls share the left pane, while the result pane stays visible at right from the start. This matches the approved Ticket Wallet sketch. Keep the flow clear through the order of the photo, edit choices, and result; omit progress labels and extra taglines. On mobile, the source image leads, the three modes stack below it, the action follows, and the result appears after completion. No flight details or travel facts are invented.
